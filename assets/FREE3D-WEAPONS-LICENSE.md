These are modified models, not the original archives. Changes include triangulation, close-vertex clustering, normalization, merged geometry, repaired normals, original muted PBR materials, and a separate magazine for reload animation. The SV98 source's ground plane was removed. The M82 was leveled and its deployed bipod removed. UI previews are vector projections of the modified meshes, generated by `tools/weapon-previews.py`.
