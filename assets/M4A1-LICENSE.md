`m4a1.glb` is a modified version of that author's OBJ: triangulated, indexed, clustered at 1.5 mm with normal boundaries preserved, merged into four meshes, scaled to 0.95 m, and given original PBR materials. The magazine remains separate for animation. The converter is in `tools/convert-m4a1.py`; it needs the original OBJ and NumPy.
