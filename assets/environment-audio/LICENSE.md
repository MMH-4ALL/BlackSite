No commercial game recordings or third-party source recordings were used.

Included: the sixteen `.wav` files listed with hashes in `manifest.json`: two
variants each of concrete, metal, dirt and gravel footsteps; separate opening
and closing door sounds; metal, crate and equipment impacts; wind, rain and
machinery loops. Mono, 22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM. Each clip is generated independently
with the seed recorded in the manifest. Quiet loops have smoothed boundaries.

Changes: synthesis, filtering, envelopes, limiting and WAV encoding, all
reproducible from the source script. These sounds supplement the existing weapon
recordings; their existing licensing and attribution are preserved.
