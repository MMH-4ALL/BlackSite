# BlackSite v0.9.0 — Tactical Tools

More tactical options and a dedicated warm-up mode, built on v0.8.1.

## Gameplay additions

- Live-fire practice range: recoil board, five reactive targets/two movers, all eight weapons, unlimited reserve with normal reloads, headshot/accuracy counters, B weapon/target/reset controls. No XP farming or career changes.
- Decoy grenades (J): $100, ten seconds of false contact; idle opponents investigate while combat and urgent objectives retain priority.
- Incendiaries (K): $500, eight-second lightweight area denial; no allied damage, wall/height-safe ticks, AI avoidance and smoke extinguishing.
- Escalating loss economy ($1,900 / $2,400 / $2,900 / $3,400 / $3,900), scoreboard credits, buy recommendations and bot rifle rebuy/sidearm fallback.
- Two-second spawn shields break on firing, utility or leaving a 3m spawn radius; small staggered bot starts preserve fair openings.
- Death recap rather than an expensive replay: killer, weapon, distance, damage and hit locations.
- Distinct original headshot/kill cues, kill confirmation, directional damage and stronger, capped surface impacts.

## Match and world tools

- First to 2/4/7/10, quick matches, custom/balanced bot fill and optional win-by-two overtime (six extra rounds then a decider).
- Match-length-aware halftime and paired overtime side switches; scores, credits and surviving weapons carry over.
- Named A/B plant/retake callouts and a small pulsing armed-core beacon.
- Helix radio relay, Bastion timed loading override, Ironwood floodlight breaker and Zero timed security bypass. Objective E priority and door occupant protection remain.

All four maps, existing weapons/models/recordings, F/Y inspection, skeletal operators,
allies, personalities, economy, objectives, buy lock/shop timer pause, skins/career,
smoke/flash, scopes, settings and Performance Mode remain. Muted native controls
fit laptop menus. No backend, account, paid APIs or new external runtime assets.

## Verification and limits

Expanded real-Three.js/GLB regression suites cover old and new gameplay. Chromium
checks use real WebGL and input under the `/BlackSite/` Pages path, including
16-bot worlds and resource cleanup. See TESTING.md for detailed results.
Software WebGL/CPU samples are not Chromebook FPS guarantees; actual hardware,
subjective audio/balance and other browsers still need human playtesting.

Download the complete ZIP, extract it and serve `index.html` with a local static
server, or play https://mmh-4all.github.io/BlackSite/. All previous releases and
asset licensing/attribution remain available.
