# BlackSite v0.8.0 — Squad Operations

BlackSite expands from solo bot rounds into offline squad operations, while preserving its eight-weapon gunplay, original maps, economy, objective rules, buy movement lock and paused shopping timer.

## Operators and teams

- Optimized animated Quaternius humanoids replace the usual procedural characters. Gray attackers, olive defenders and ally markers keep teams distinguishable.
- Thirteen skeletal clips with blended movement/upper-body handling, crouch, aim, recoil, reload, flash/objective reactions and deaths. Rifles follow the hand while aiming toward the actual target; animation never drives damage or navigation.
- Six decision personalities: Aggressive, Cautious, Flanker, Objective, Marksman and Support. Easy/Normal/Hard retain their existing reaction/aim/memory values.
- Select 0–7 friendly bots against 1–16 enemies, including team presets. Allies fight, navigate, plant, defend and defuse, and can finish a round after the human dies. Friendly fire is disabled.
- Automatic side switch after round three, with clear UI, new spawns/objective roles and retained score, credits and surviving equipment.

## Tactical environments

- All three existing maps remain. Communications props and warm desert dust strengthen Helix; cargo/loading machinery strengthens Bastion; cold light rain and motor-pool equipment strengthen Ironwood.
- New original **BlackSite Zero**: a partially underground military research installation with two sites, security/service routes, research rooms, exterior access, doors and an accessible raised position.
- Sliding doors animate and play local audio, block bullets/sight/collision when closed, protect occupants and can be opened by bots. **E** interacts; planting/defusing takes priority.
- Height-aware cached navigation supports reliable textured ramps and gantries. The one-height-per-X/Z design deliberately avoids unreliable stacked floors/ladders; decorative perimeter towers remain inaccessible.
- Subtle atmosphere scales with quality. Small breakable lights/equipment produce capped temporary debris and reset each round, without changing navigation or objective collision.

## Career, cosmetics and interface

- Offline XP/levels, ten permanent challenges, service badges, personal bests, map/difficulty/weapon statistics and a Career screen, saved locally without an account or backend.
- Nine original Armory finishes across all eight weapons: Default, Tactical Black, Desert, Urban, Woodland, Carbon, Worn Steel, BlackSite Elite and Field Honors. Four are available from level one; others are cosmetic unlocks. No skin or level changes weapon power.
- Reports include result/score, setup, side, combat/objectives, accuracy, XP, level progress, challenge/unlock progress and round history.
- Team setup, allies/enemies remaining, expanded scoreboard, staged asset loading and graceful optional-character/storage failures.
- Crosshair length, thickness, gap, dot, outline, opacity, shape/presets and optional dynamic expansion are saved.
- Sixteen distinct original local CC0 environment sounds add surface footsteps, doors, impacts and ambient wind/rain/machinery. Existing sixty recorded weapon sounds are preserved.

## Performance and preservation

- One skinned draw per operator, shared simplified bot rifle, batched static scenery, staggered senses/path work, cached navigation and animation distance LOD. Performance Mode disables shadows, weather particles and debris.
- All assets/modules remain bundled, relative and GitHub Pages compatible. No runtime CDN, paid API, backend or login is introduced.
- Preserves weapons/animations/audio, crouch/jump/scopes/recoil/inspect, smoke/flash, difficulty, economy, planting/defusing, Armory, settings, HUD/scoreboard and the v0.7.1 floor-flicker fix.
- Added CC0 Quaternius/Kenney credits, original source notices and reproducible asset tools. Existing separately licensed Free3D/CC-BY assets retain their permissions and attribution.

## Validation and current limits

All five suites passed again on the final build: gameplay/model/animation, career/cosmetics/UI, environment, existing weapon audio and real Chromium WebGL/input. Covered all four maps/both sides, Easy/Normal/Hard, 1/6/16 enemies, maximum 23-bot teams, objectives, side switching, doors/ramps, purchases/weapon retention, smoke/flash, all 72 weapon/finish shader combinations, storage failures, map cleanup and the `/BlackSite/` path. Browser console/runtime errors and external runtime requests were absent.

With sixteen bots, Performance Mode rendered **86–100 world draw calls** across the four maps. Repeated map resets held geometry/texture counts stable. These are software WebGL/CPU measurements, **not Chromebook hardware FPS**. Real-device sustained performance, speaker playback, cross-browser behavior and subjective match balance still need player testing. This remains one human plus AI, without online multiplayer. Full commands, measured budgets and technical limits are in `TESTING.md` and `tools/reports/v0.8.0-validation.json`.
